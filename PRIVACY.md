# Privacy Policy

Battery Flyout respects your privacy. The app does not collect, store, or send any personal user data.

## Data collected by the app

Battery Flyout may collect technical and diagnostic data related to the device and the app installation. This may include:

- Windows version
- battery information
- processor information
- installed app version
- app usage and diagnostic data from approximately the last 30 days

This data is technical in nature and does not include personal information about the user.

## Local storage

All collected diagnostic and usage data is stored locally on the device where Battery Flyout is installed.

Battery Flyout does not automatically upload diagnostic files or send them to the publisher.

## Changes to this policy

The publisher may modify or update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Users are encouraged to review this document periodically.
